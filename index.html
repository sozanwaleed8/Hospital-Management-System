about us

The hospital contains a variety of specialized departments, such as the emergency department, internal medicine department, pediatric department, and others.

A

Children’s specialist

A pediatrician is an essential source of support for families, providing advice on proper nutrition, vaccinations, disease prevention, and age-appropriate health practices. He or she also regularly monitors the child's physical and mental development to ensure healthy growth.

B

Internal Medicine Specialist

An internist is distinguished by her ability to evaluate complex cases that require a comprehensive study of symptoms and linking them to their causes. She also provides preventive advice to promote general health, such as nutritional guidelines, weight management, and prevention of common diseases.

C

Cardiologist

A cardiologist performs advanced tests such as electrocardiograms (ECGs), echocardiograms, and stress tests to assess heart health and accurately diagnose problems. He also provides advice on preventing heart disease, including lifestyle changes, healthy diet, and exercise.
